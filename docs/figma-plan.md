# Figma plan — create these frames yourself

Create three desktop frames (1440 px wide) and three mobile frames (390 px wide).

## Visual system
- Colors: paper `#F6F3EB`, ink `#202C25`, green `#315B43`, dark green `#203D2C`, lime `#D7ED8A`, white `#FFFEFA`.
- Type: Georgia for large editorial headings; Arial/Helvetica for body text.
- Spacing: use an 8 px base rhythm (8, 16, 24, 32, 48, 80 px).
- Buttons: dark green with white text; clear hover/focus state.

## Frame 1 — Home
1. Header: brand left, Stories / Featured story / About & access navigation right.
2. Hero: left text, large heading “Big ideas start somewhere.” and CTA; right founder/team photo.
3. Lime strip with three short statements.
4. “Meet the dreamers” section with three story cards.
5. Dark green quote band.
6. Final CTA and footer.

## Frame 2 — Story detail
1. Shared header.
2. Centered title, category, summary, and sample byline.
3. Wide image and caption.
4. Two-column content: article and “Listen / Read” transcript panel.
5. Takeaway box and related stories link.
6. Footer.

## Frame 3 — About / Access
1. Shared header.
2. Large mission headline and wide team photo.
3. Two-column mission section.
4. Four accessibility feature cards.
5. Contact / contribution CTA.
6. Footer.

## Mobile variants
Stack the hero, story cards, article and transcript panel, mission section, and access cards into one column. Keep text at a comfortable size and ensure buttons are easy to tap.

Add your Figma file URL to the README after you have made the frames. These instructions are not a substitute for actually creating the Figma mockups.
