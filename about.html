WHY WE TELL THESE STORIES

Progress is a story, not a finish line.

Dream in Progress shares the honest early chapters of young entrepreneurs: the questions, experiments, setbacks, and small wins that rarely fit into a success headline.

OUR MISSION

Make the beginning feel possible.

Entrepreneurship can look intimidating when we only see polished outcomes. We focus on the process behind the idea so young people can learn from practical choices, mistakes, and lessons.

This student project prototype uses sample content to demonstrate accessible storytelling. Real stories should be shared with permission and represented accurately.

DESIGNED FOR MORE PEOPLE
